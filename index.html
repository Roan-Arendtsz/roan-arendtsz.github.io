About

I am a fourth-year undergraduate student at Carleton College. I am interested in elementary descriptions of the universe, in particular string theory and other theories pertaining to the quantum gravity project. There are several subtopics which are of the most interst to me. First, extending the vast array of tools developed from the AdS/CFT duality to new contexts. Second, the problem of the bulk gravitational partition function. Third, the cosmological constant problem, and more generally the integration of dark energy and dark matter into the standard model. Exercise Complilations

I have found that the most direct way to learn physics is to work on problems. There were several courses throughout my undergraduate education which I was unable to take for various reasons, and which in hindsight I wish I had taken. Spurred by a desire to fulfill this gap in my education, I have compiled a series of exercises which I have worked out by myself. These projects all remain in progress, and will be updated as I have time to work on the exercises. The topics which I have chosen are Classical Mechanics, Complex Analysis, and Lie Groups.
