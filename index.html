Exercise Complilations

I have found that the most direct way to learn physics is to work on problems. There were several courses throughout my undergraduate education which I was unable to take for various reasons, and which in hindsight I wish I had taken. Spurred by a desire to fulfill this gap in my education, I have compiled a series of exercises which I have worked out by myself. These projects all remain in progress, and will be updated as I have time to work on the exercises. The topics which I have chosen are Classical Mechanics, Complex Analysis, and Lie Groups.
